# Closed-loop executor checkpoint

The offline executor and onboard mailbox are implemented as a **gated prototype**, not a hardware-ready closed-loop controller. Shadow remains the default. The bridge reports `semantic_execution_enabled: false` even if intent admission is configured on; the moving-eyes runner refuses supervised mode in that state. Admitted semantic motion is rejected by an additional onboard interlock. The 285-test offline suite passed on November 7, 2026; this does not establish physical safety or a functioning moving perception loop. The new source was not deployed.

Two independent reviews exposed safety issues. Host-side transport checks and onboard executor-generation fencing are now implemented and exercised with fake I/O, including cancellation races; these do **not** establish that a TCP write already in progress can be revoked after cancellation. The semantic execution interlock stays closed until that limitation is resolved and independently reviewed. Visual `look` is not treated as permission for a translating curve. Stationary look remains unsupported. Uncertain delivery that expires absent from the observed mailbox is recorded as unconfirmed, not successful.

The source version is `0.20.5-semantic-intents`. On November 7, 2026, BEEP was reachable directly via DOGZILLA_WIFI and Tailscale. Live bridge remained `0.20.3-vendor-app-reverse`, stopped with no mission or lease. Direct V4L2 capture produced a decodable 640×480 room frame without starting the vendor motor/app service. The live `/scan` topic advertised **zero publishers**, scan age intermittently exceeded the 0.6-second guard, the pose guard reported `stationary_drift`, SLAM was unusable, and the vendor camera/app service was inactive. Cartographer's running process and log scan-rate reports do not override these observed readiness failures. No motion, service restart, or deployment was performed; recheck live state before any future trial. The object named in the handoff was a replaceable perception test target, not the objective.
