The source bridge is now `0.21.0-stop-priority`, but **was not deployed to BEEP**; the last confirmed deployed bridge is `0.20.9-steering-sweep`. ## Implemented and replayed

- `beep_agent/visual_grounding.py` consumes Phase A `EventEnvelope` and `project_capture()` without migrating the journal schema. A verified sensor-exposure stamp, capture-time valid local TF, matching boot/epoch/revision/snapshot in a current optimized map transform, matching frame/evidence IDs, sufficient detection confidence, a normalized bounding box, and a matching camera-posture calibration are required. Output is a **map-frame uncertain viewpoint cone** from the calibrated camera mount, with no target distance, point coordinate, or motor authority. Stationary observation needs no motion lease.
- `ViewpointRequest` admission is pure and advisory, requiring *current* session/boot/epoch/revision/snapshot, exact active mission and lease, matching source evidence and frame time, a bearing inside the cone, and **two separate robot-clock windows**: acquisition evidence no older than 10 seconds, and a post-inference request lifetime `0 < expires_at_robot - issued_at_robot <= 2 seconds`. The caller must stamp `issued_at_robot` from the robot clock, not a model-generated wall-clock value; it must lie between acquisition and validation time. Phase C grounding accepts an optimized transform up to 10 seconds after exposure only with the exact matching map context. A stationary READY/between-lease frame `(mission_id=None, lease_id=None)` can be used once a fresh request binds the current lease; an observation owned by an old lease cannot. It does **not** validate a path, obstacle clearance, physical camera calibration, map->odom producer, or collision envelope. It cannot move the robot.
- Audit corrections after `7897790`: use pinhole projection of **both** bounding-box edges, not linear pixel-to-angle interpolation; expand the cone by measured local SLAM yaw uncertainty and retain origin XY uncertainty separately. Unknown uncertainty remains explicitly `None`, not a measured zero. A cone wider than a hemisphere is rejected. Distinct moving-camera rays remain anchored to their separate exposure-time origins; there is no two-view triangulation or inferred target distance in this slice.
- The historical cap packet only records a qualitative `right` position and a frame receipt/capture field whose sensor-exposure provenance is unverified. It cannot yield a metric target position or a valid cone; synthetic bbox/calibration tests exercise the contract without presenting fixture metadata as measured hardware truth.
- `beep_agent/viewpoint_scorer.py` consumes only a measured-uncertainty cone and a bounded map occupancy snapshot whose boot/epoch/revision/snapshot matches the **current** verified map context. It checks known-free cells across the capture-origin wedge in a single bounded scan and checks the candidate footprint, rejects unknown or occupied cells, and returns a possible inspection viewpoint with its bearing computed from the **current** pose—not the old camera heading. It does not identify object range, prove a route from current pose, or dispatch motors. The conservative known-free wedge check is synthetic geometry; real map quality and clearance still need validation.
- `beep_bridge/progress_monitor.py` implements a side-effect-free `MotionDiscrepancy`: same-identity, **explicitly verified map revision**, sufficient straight-gait cycles and corroborated pose/range/visual-motion windows; two contiguous weak windows are required for `stalled`. A visual/range-versus-pose conflict is flagged separately. `possible_fall` requires an external attitude alert and is not a roll certification or battery estimate. No live producer or motor action is connected.
- The source app transport now performs owned motion connect and greeting **outside** `app_io_lock`, then rechecks exact authority before any packets. An `app_transition_lock` preserves the release→restart sequence for ordinary motion while allowing a stop to bypass a stalled connect, greeting or straighten transition. State publication remains under the register-write lock, preventing a late moving-state overwrite. Event-controlled tests prove the host stop packet can complete before a blocked motion connect/greeting resumes and prove replacement-owner fencing. An already-entered blocked write still holds the write lock; the fake transport cannot establish vendor consumption or physical stopping distance.
- The original witness showed cancellation latching while stop waited on a blocked greeting. The new tests convert that case to a stop-priority pass **only for the host fake-socket connect/greeting boundary**; they retain a separate in-flight-write limitation witness. Stop's own connect/greeting/`sendall`, write-lock wait and physical servo response remain unbounded and must not be described as solved.
