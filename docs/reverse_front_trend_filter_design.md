# Proposed reverse front-trend filter — design only

**Status:** offline design; not implemented, deployed, or authorized for motion. The Pi still runs `0.22.2-stack2-greeting-optional` with the original 0.02 m front-loss guard and 5° heading guard.

## Evidence and scope

`scan_callback()` reports the **minimum valid ray** in each of six angular sectors. `supervise_lidar_motion()` currently averages the three front sector minima and aborts reverse as soon as that one-snapshot mean falls more than `BACKOFF_MAX_FRONT_LOSS_M=0.02 m` from its pre-command snapshot. This is a *relative trend* check, not the same as the rear/side absolute collision envelope. In one stopped five-second 20 Hz status series, that mean was >0.02 m below the first sample in 17/100 polls; front-right varied about 1.173–1.281 m. These polls are not 100 independent scans: `/status` does not change every 50 ms, and the sampled series was not retained. The result shows this baseline can trigger while stopped; it is **not** a general 17% LiDAR error rate or proof of multipath/stance motion.

Two physically supervised reverse requests on `0.22.2` sent `app_send backward`. The unchanged trend check stopped them after 0.080 s and 0.405 s of supervision. The operator saw no movement, then body movement without travel. Neither is evidence of useful reverse translation. Do not tune the motor command, loosen 0.02 m, or retry to compensate for this sensing issue.

## Candidate architecture

1. Preserve **raw, immediate** validity and authority: exact lease/cancellation/deadline, fresh scan, all six finite positive sectors, usable SLAM, raw rear and side absolute floors, and the existing 5° yaw guard. No smoothed reading may authorize movement across an absolute envelope breach. Keep stop priority and the existing motion ceiling. Add a separately reviewed raw front/diagonal *absolute* hard-contact floor before any filtered reverse trial; it must admit only measured safe starting geometry, not silently replace or weaken the current relative guard.
2. In the scan callback, retain a small bounded ring of **distinct** scan IDs with robot-monotonic time and the three front sector minima. Never count repeated HTTP/status polls or repeated supervisor snapshots as independent evidence; clear/reject the ring across restart, stale/missing/invalid scans, or boot identity change. Keep the raw six-sector snapshot alongside it.
3. Before `motor_send`, require five fresh, distinct, valid *stationary* scans spanning a short measured window, with no pose jump or active motion. Fix the baseline to the median of the five per-scan front-triplet means. Do not adapt the baseline while moving; otherwise slow real approach could be normalized away. If the baseline cannot be established inside a bounded time, refuse motion rather than improvising a single frame. Account for this preflight in the owner lease/deadline.
4. For each **new** scan during reverse, compute the raw front-triplet mean. Seed a three-value window with one fixed-baseline representative; append only distinct new scan means, retaining at most three values. Compare the median only once the window has three values against the fixed baseline at the **same 0.02 m limit**. Thus a single new low sector-minimum spike cannot abort the *trend* check, but two persistent new bad scans do. This adds up to a scan interval plus polling latency (measure it; do not assume exactly 10 Hz). It deliberately delays this one trend check, so preserve a separately reviewed immediate raw front-center loss veto and raw hard-contact floor; obvious frontal approach must never wait for the median.
5. `stop_on_front_gain_m` must use the same fixed baseline and require a confirmed filtered gain; a single high return cannot declare recovery success. A `window_complete` with no confirmed gain is still a failed autonomous reverse escape. Log raw triplet, filtered triplet, scan IDs, baseline, consecutive evidence, and exact stop reason for audit. Keep the per-packet safety guards, rear/side hard floors, and yaw abort outside the filter.
6. Use the same helper for `back`/`backward` and reverse arcs if those paths are ever enabled. **Do not** globally smooth `sector_values()` or the local forward/turn collision checks: that would mask real obstacles for unrelated behaviors.

## Before any Pi deployment or motion

- **Dataset choice:** `captures/` is mostly JPEG/observation-packet material. Some packets contain `scan_count` and six-sector status, but they are sparse mission snapshots rather than a contiguous ~10 Hz scan stream. The two recent 20 Hz supervised traces are in profile scratch (`beep-stage2-reverse-baseline-v2.json` and `-v3.json`); they record sectors and moving flags but omit `scan_count`. Use these as provenance and regression examples, **not** as proof of distinct-scan filtering. Do not concatenate snapshots from different boots, placements, versions or motion phases into one synthetic timeline.
- **Capture format:** collect a dedicated read-only, on-Pi JSONL sequence from the ROS `/scan` callback (or the bridge's scan callback) with one row per scan: `boot_id`, monotonic capture time, `scan_count`, six raw sector minima, scan validity, guarded/raw yaw, and a stationary/moving annotation. For a spatial filter experiment, retain bounded angular raw-ray samples separately; sector minima alone cannot reconstruct which ray produced a dip. Start with stationary runs at several placements and no motor commands. Do not run another reverse merely to fill a dataset.
- **Replay structure:** feed rows through the unchanged guard and each candidate in strict timestamp/scan-ID order; reject missing, duplicate, out-of-order or stale rows; preserve unfiltered rear/side and raw hard-contact checks. Label real captures separately from synthetic hazard injections. Report each decision, triplet baseline/median, first-stop scan/time, missed hazards, and extra motor-on latency. Inject a sudden rear obstruction on the next scan: the raw rear threshold must stop immediately, irrespective of the front median window. Inject a persistent worsening front and compare its delayed detection against the old guard under a conservative measured stopping-distance budget.
- Build a pure deterministic helper and tests first: isolated low ray, alternating low readings, two/three persistent worsening scans, real rear/side/diagonal hard-floor breach, missing/stale/non-finite sectors, repeated scan ID, cancellation/stop during confirmation, yaw drift, and spurious front gain. Replay recorded stationary and supervised traces with raw and candidate decisions side by side. Report false-abort count **and** worst-case extra stop latency; do not optimize only for fewer aborts.
- Keep autonomous reverse disabled until a separate physically supervised trial shows actual translation and confirms the complete swept-body and stop response. A host-test pass or fewer false positives is not commissioning. Never call `/move` to bypass this supervisor.

**Decision gate:** choose the filter/window only after the scan-level replay shows that stationary single-ray spikes are rejected while sustained approach and hard-envelope breaches still stop promptly. If the latency/geometry cannot be defended, keep the current fail-closed guard and investigate the sensor geometry or vendor gait protocol instead.
